Account & Data

Delete Your Account

Last updated: September 25, 2026

1. About this page

This page explains how to delete your MyCommunities account (Android, iOS, and web) and what happens to the data associated with it. MyCommunities is the app published by the MyCommunities team (package name io.mycommunities.app).

Account deletion is available from inside the app and also without installing the app, by email. Both routes delete the same data.

2. Delete your account in the app

If you can still sign in, this is the fastest route:

  1. Open the MyCommunities app and sign in with your account.
  2. Tap the ⚙ Account Settings icon in the top bar (or go to Settings).
  3. Scroll to the Danger Zone section and open Account Deletion.
  4. Optionally type a reason, then tap Request Deletion.
  5. In the confirmation dialog, type DELETE and confirm.

Your request is recorded immediately and reviewed by a platform administrator. While a request is pending you can undo it at any time by tapping Cancel Deletion Request on the same screen. Once a request is approved the deletion is permanent and cannot be reversed.

3. Delete your account without the app

If you cannot sign in, or you no longer have the app installed, email us from the address registered to the account:

Email: support@mycommunities.app
Subject: Account Deletion Request

Please include the account email and, if you have one, the name of the community you belong to. We may ask you to confirm ownership of the account before we act, to protect you from fraudulent deletion requests.

4. How long it takes

We process verified deletion requests within 30 days of receiving them, and in most cases much sooner. In-app requests are reviewed in the order they arrive. You will not be able to sign in to the account once deletion is complete.

5. What data is deleted

When your account is deleted, your account and personal data are removed from the MyCommunities platform, including:

  • Your account and profile — name, email address, phone number, photo, address, preferred language, and community memberships.
  • Content you authored — discussion posts, comments, announcements, and the content of your chat messages (messages are replaced with "[message deleted]" so other participants' threads stay readable).
  • Transactions and requests — tickets and ticket comments, visitor records, amenity bookings, marketplace listings and their products, service bookings where you were the buyer or seller, and reviews.
  • Notifications, notification read state, and other records tied to your user ID.

6. What may be retained

A limited amount of information is kept after deletion, only where we are required or permitted to do so:

  • Security and audit records — an append-only audit entry recording that the account was deleted (account identifier, email, date, and action), kept for audit, fraud-prevention, and security purposes.
  • Records required by law — financial, tax, or transaction records, and any information we must retain to comply with legal obligations or to resolve disputes.
  • Aggregated or anonymised data — statistics that no longer identify you.

Information you shared inside a community may remain visible to other members in copies already made by them, and community administrators may retain community-level records that are not tied to your account, as described in our Privacy Policy (Section 6).

7. Contact us

Questions about deleting your account or your data? Email support@mycommunities.app and we will respond as promptly as possible.
