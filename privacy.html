Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

1. Introduction

MyCommunities ("we", "us", or "our") operates the MyCommunities platform, a community management service for residential communities, homeowners associations, condominiums, villages, dormitories, and similar organizations. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our website, mobile application, and related services (collectively, the "Service").

By creating an account, accessing, or using the Service, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy and consent to the collection, use, and sharing of your information as described below. If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not use the Service.

2. Information We Collect

Account Information: When you register, we collect your name, email address, phone number, account type, and password (stored securely and never exposed in plain text).

Profile Information: Information you choose to add to your profile, such as your photo, address, preferred language, and community memberships.

Community Data: Information you provide in connection with a community, such as discussion posts, comments, announcements, tickets, visitor records, amenity bookings, marketplace listings, transactions, and messages.

Proof of Authority & Verification Data: If you request to create or administer a community, we may collect contact information, proof of authority documents, and related verification data.

Usage Data: Information about how you interact with the Service, including pages visited, features used, and device information such as browser type, operating system, and IP address.

Authentication Data: If you sign in using a third-party provider (such as Google or Apple), we receive the profile information that provider makes available to us in accordance with your provider account settings.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

(a) provide, operate, and maintain the Service and its features; (b) create and manage your account and community memberships; (c) process community requests, verifications, bookings, and marketplace transactions; (d) enable messaging and notifications between you and other users; (e) improve, personalize, and secure the Service; (f) communicate with you about your account, the Service, and important updates; (g) respond to your inquiries and provide support; (h) detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, security, or technical issues; and (i) comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information only as described in this Privacy Policy:

(a) Within your communities: information you post or provide in a community is visible to other members of that community in accordance with the community's role-based permissions; (b) With service providers: we may share information with trusted vendors who help us operate the Service (e.g., hosting, analytics, authentication) under confidentiality obligations; (c) For legal reasons: we may disclose information when required by law, subpoena, or legal process, or when we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, your safety, or the safety of others; (d) Business transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

5. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When you delete your account, we will take reasonable steps to delete or anonymize your personal information, except where we are required to retain it by law or for legitimate business purposes such as fraud prevention and audit records.

6. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, update, or delete your personal information; to object to or restrict certain processing; to request a copy of your data; and to withdraw consent at any time. You can update much of your profile information directly in the Service. To exercise your rights, contact us using the details in Section 10.

Account deletion: You may request deletion of your account. Please note that some information you shared within communities may remain visible to other members as permitted by those communities.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include encryption in transit, secure authentication, and role-based access controls. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We may use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember your preferences, and understand how the Service is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings, but disabling them may affect the functionality of the Service.

9. Third-Party Services and Links

The Service may integrate with or contain links to third-party services (such as authentication providers, payment processors, or analytics tools). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party service you use.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us at support@mycommunities.app. We will respond to your inquiry as promptly as possible.
